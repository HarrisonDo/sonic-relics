Sonic Relics — original demo sketches

These six instrumental sketches are generated by the original deterministic
synthesis/composition script at scripts/generate-demo-audio.mjs. They do not
contain sampled recordings or third-party melodies.

The sketches and generated artwork are dedicated to the public domain under CC0 1.0:
https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/

The years displayed for demo tracks are fictional listening-scene dates,
not historical release dates. The app labels them as demo scenes.
