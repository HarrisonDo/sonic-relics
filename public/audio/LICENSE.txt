Sonic Relics — original demo sketches

These seven instrumental sketches are generated by the original deterministic
synthesis/composition script at scripts/generate-demo-audio.mjs. They do not
contain sampled recordings or third-party melodies. The record-surface noise in
vinyl.wav is synthesised by the same script and is part of that sketch; the app
never adds it to imported music.

The following pre-existing audio and artwork were dedicated to the public domain
under CC0 1.0. Those dedications are not withdrawn; these assets remain CC0:
